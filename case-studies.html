Case study 1

Building Website Professional Services

As the Website Specialist, I created and managed a new paid service offering from scratch, building the processes, pricing, and tracking while handling every client request as a solo contributor.

Challenge

The company had no structured or monetized approach to website services. Projects were managed inconsistently, which hurt efficiency and visibility and left revenue on the table.

Impact

  • Established a replicable, revenue-generating service line.
  • Improved client experience with standardized workflows and visibility.
  • Translated stakeholder needs into actionable business strategy.

Approach

  • Benchmarked competitors' pricing and service tiers through market research.
  • Designed tiered packages aligned with client needs and revenue goals.
  • Built workflows and SOPs across Confluence, Zendesk, and SharePoint.
  • Developed an Excel-based system to track project statuses and requests.
  • Used AI diagnostics to solve process and technical issues such as email delivery and ticketing automation.
  • Confluence
  • Zendesk
  • SharePoint
  • Excel
  • AI diagnostics

Case study 2

AI-Assisted Problem Solving Across Roles

Across several roles, I used AI tools to diagnose technical and process problems, giving internal teams actionable findings without waiting on engineering.

Challenge

Recurring technical errors and inefficient workflows slowed teams down, and escalated fixes were often deprioritized by engineering:

  • CRM emails failing DKIM verification.
  • Zendesk triggers misfiring and overwriting initial client communications.
  • Jira priorities misused, with agents overusing "highest."
  • Content generation bottlenecks in SaaS implementations.

Impact

  • Produced actionable technical recommendations, some escalated to engineering for review.
  • Prevented client communication breakdowns by finding Zendesk workarounds.
  • Bridged business, technical, and AI tools to solve complex problems quickly.

Approach

  • Ran AI-driven diagnostics on email headers, uncovering DKIM body hash mismatches and showing that Lofty's system was altering email content after signing.
  • Troubleshot Zendesk triggers with AI and identified workarounds that stop template macros from replacing initial client emails.
  • Flagged Jira prioritization misuse and proposed new classification guidelines and escalation paths.
  • ChatGPT
  • Gemini
  • Zendesk
  • Jira
  • Email header analysis

Case study 3

Stakeholder Communication & Business Case Development

While leading Website Professional Services, I saw the need to scale beyond a one-person operation and built a business case for expansion, reframing website services as a revenue-generating business line for executives.

Challenge

There was no scalable structure for website services and no clear strategy for monetization or resourcing. I had proven the model as a one-person team, but growth needed leadership buy-in and a clear case for the revenue opportunity.

Impact

  • Translated operational work into strategic value for leadership.
  • Positioned Website Professional Services as a scalable business line rather than an ad hoc offering.
  • Showed stakeholder communication, business case development, and strategic planning skills central to a business analyst role.

Approach

  • Researched competitors' service tiers and pricing to set benchmarks.
  • Designed tiered packages aligned with client needs and revenue growth.
  • Created visual mockups and process diagrams to show how the service could scale.
  • Built a pitch deck around potential revenue, resource requirements, and risk mitigation.
  • Presented to leadership, tailoring the message for technical and sales stakeholders.
  • Market research
  • Pricing strategy
  • Executive presentation
  • Process diagrams
